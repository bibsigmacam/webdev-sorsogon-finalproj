About the Province

The Southernmost Province of Luzon

Sorsogon is the southernmost province of Luzon, nestled at the tip of the Bicol Peninsula and bounded by the Pacific Ocean to the east, San Bernardino Strait to the south, and the Ticao Pass to the west — a land of unmatched natural beauty.

Beyond its star attraction, the province boasts volcanic peaks, hidden lakes, colonial churches, and rich cultural traditions.
