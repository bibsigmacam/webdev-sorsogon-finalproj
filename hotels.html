Hotels & Accommodation

Stay in Sorsogon

Accommodation for Every Traveller

From comfortable city hotels in Sorsogon City to rustic eco-lodges in Donsol and beachside guesthouses in Matnog, the province offers accommodation suited to all budgets and travel styles.

Capital Hotels

Full-service mid-range hotels, transit inns, and apartelles in Sorsogon City — perfect base for exploring the whole province.

Donsol Eco-Lodges

Sustainable lodging near whale shark waters with integrated tour packages — wake up steps from the bay.

Matnog Beach Camps

Budget-friendly beachside homestays and organized tent campgrounds for island-hopping adventurers.

Mountain Retreats

Cool highland lodges near Irosin and Bulusan — ideal for volcano trekkers and nature lovers seeking fresh mountain air.

📅 Strategic Booking Notice

During peak whale shark months (February–April) and the Kasanggayahan Festival season (October), accommodations across all zones book up rapidly. Secure reservations 4–6 weeks in advance. Contact the tourism office for official lodging referrals and package rates.
